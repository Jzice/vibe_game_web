Frostfall

A first-person Nordic RPG: a frozen valley, a crypt, a giant and a dragon to kill.

Game·Plays in your browser·Nov 10, 2026

Play Frostfall

Playfull screen

An open valley about a mile across that you walk in first person: a river village with a smith and a mead hall, a pine forest with wolves, a barrow full of the restless dead, a bandit watchtower, a frozen lake with a giant, a waterfall with something behind it and a shrine on a peak. Sword, shield, fire in your left hand and a Shout that throws enemies back. The dragon comes for the village at the end, in three phases. Day and night, weather and generated sound, in the browser on a desktop or a phone.

Goal & tips

Goal Speak to the elder, arm yourself, take the Word from the crypt under Kings' Barrow, then kill the dragon when it comes for the village.

Try this
  • Every enemy winds up before it strikes. Raise the shield or step out, then hit back.
  • Hold the attack for a power attack. Sneak up on someone who has not seen you for triple damage.
  • Hold Q for a stream of flames, tap it for a fireball. Burning enemies keep burning.
  • The giant by the frozen lake leaves you alone until you hit him. Then he hits you.
